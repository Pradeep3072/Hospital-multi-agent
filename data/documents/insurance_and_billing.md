# HopeCare General Hospital - Insurance, Pricing & Billing Policy

## Accepted Insurance Networks
HopeCare General Hospital participates in the preferred provider networks for the following major healthcare insurance providers:
1. **Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS)**: All PPO and HMO plans accepted. Prior authorization may be required for elective surgical procedures.
2. **Aetna Healthcare**: Tier 1 in-network provider for Open Access and Managed Choice plans.
3. **UnitedHealthcare (UHC)**: Choice Plus and Navigate network plans fully recognized.
4. **Cigna Global Health**: In-network for Open Access Plus (OAP) and Network POS plans.
5. **Medicare & Medicaid**: Full coverage for eligible Part A and Part B hospital services, subject to statutory deductibles and copayments.

## Out-of-Network and Self-Pay Patients
- Patients without insurance or whose insurance is out-of-network are entitled to a Good Faith Estimate under the No Surprises Act.
- A 25% prompt-pay discount is applied to hospital facility charges when paid within 14 days of billing.
- Need-based financial assistance programs are available for households with income up to 300% of federal poverty guidelines.
