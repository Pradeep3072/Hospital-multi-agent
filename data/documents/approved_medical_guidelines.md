# HopeCare General Hospital - Approved Patient Health Guidelines

## Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) Management
- **Monitoring**: Check blood pressure at the same time each morning and evening before meals. Record readings in your health log.
- **Diet**: Adopt the DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension) diet, prioritizing leafy greens, whole grains, and lean proteins while keeping sodium intake below 2,000 mg/day.
- **Medication Compliance**: Take prescribed antihypertensive medications (e.g., Lisinopril, Amlodipine) consistently as instructed by your doctor even if you feel completely fine. Do not discontinue without clinical consultation.
- **Red Flag Symptoms**: Seek immediate emergency medical care if systolic blood pressure exceeds 180 or diastolic exceeds 120, or if high blood pressure is accompanied by chest pain, shortness of breath, back pain, numbness/weakness, or visual disturbances.

## General Post-Operative Wound Care
- Keep surgical incisions clean, dry, and covered with sterile dressings for the first 48 hours unless directed otherwise by your surgical team.
- Wash hands thoroughly with antimicrobial soap before touching dressings.
- Normal healing may involve mild redness, slight itching, and clear or pinkish drainage.
- **Warning Signs of Infection**: Contact the surgical clinic immediately if you experience worsening redness spreading from the incision, purulent/cloudy discharge with an odor, persistent fever over 101°F (38.3°C), or increasing localized severe pain.

## Type 2 Diabetes Self-Care Essentials
- Check fasting blood glucose regularly. Maintain target levels as discussed with your physician (typically 80-130 mg/dL fasting, and under 180 mg/dL two hours post-meal).
- Inspect feet daily for cuts, blisters, red spots, or swelling. Always wear comfortable, protective footwear.
- Carry quick-acting carbohydrates (glucose tablets or 4 oz fruit juice) for episodes of hypoglycemia (blood sugar below 70 mg/dL).
