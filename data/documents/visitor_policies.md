# HopeCare General Hospital - Visitor & Safety Regulations

## Infection Prevention & Hygiene Standards
- All visitors must clean their hands using antiseptic foam or soap and water upon entering and leaving patient care areas.
- Masking is optional in general waiting areas, but strictly required in the Oncology Ward, Bone Marrow Transplant unit, and Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU).
- Anyone experiencing flu-like symptoms, fever, active cough, or rash is requested to refrain from visiting inpatients until symptom-free for at least 48 hours.

## Pediatric & Maternity Ward Visitation
- **Pediatrics**: Parents or legal guardians may stay with hospitalized children 24 hours a day. One pull-out sleeper chair is provided per pediatric private room.
- **Maternity & Labor/Delivery**: One primary designated birthing partner has 24/7 access during active labor and postpartum recovery. Additional visitors are welcomed during standard ward hours (10:00 AM - 8:00 PM).

## Quiet Hours & Patient Rest
- Hospital-wide Quiet Hours are observed from 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM to facilitate patient healing and restful sleep. Overhead paging is minimized, and hallway lighting is dimmed.
