# HopeCare General Hospital - Visitor & Facility Guide

## Hospital Timings & Visiting Hours
- **General Inpatient Wards**: Daily from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Maximum of 2 visitors at any time per bedside.
- **Intensive Care Unit (ICU / CCU)**: Visiting hours are restricted to:
  - Afternoon: 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM
  - Evening: 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM
  - Only immediate family members (maximum 1 at a time).
- **Outpatient Clinics (OPD)**: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM; Saturday, 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- **Emergency Department & Trauma Center**: Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

## Facilities & Amenities
- **Main Pharmacy**: Located in Building A, Ground Floor, near the main entrance lobby. Open 24/7 for prescriptions and over-the-counter medicine. Contact: ext. 4410.
- **Hospital Cafeteria (The Garden Cafe)**: Located in Building B, Ground Floor.
  - Breakfast: 6:30 AM - 10:00 AM
  - Lunch: 11:30 AM - 3:00 PM
  - Dinner: 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM
- **Coffee Shop (Espresso Corner)**: Main Lobby, Building A. Open 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.
- **Parking Information**:
  - Visitor Parking Lot C (surface lot) and Garage B (underground).
  - Rates: Free for the first 30 minutes; $3.00 per hour thereafter; Maximum daily cap: $15.00.
  - Valet parking is available at the Main Entrance of Building A for $8 flat fee.
- **ATM & Banking**: Located adjacent to the Main Pharmacy in Building A.
- **Chapel & Meditation Room**: Building A, 2nd Floor, open 24/7 for all faiths and contemplation.
- **Lost and Found**: Contact Security Office at Building A, Lower Level or call ext. 4099.
