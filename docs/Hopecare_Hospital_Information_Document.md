## 7. Appointment & Admission Workflow

1. **Selection:** Patient selects department/doctor and preferred appointment slot.
2. **Verification:** Reception or digital system verifies patient details and appointment availability.
3. **Registration:** Patient completes registration and provides required identification/insurance information.
4. **Consultation:** Doctor consultation is completed and investigations are ordered when clinically required.
5. **Admission (if needed):** Hospital confirms bed/room availability and completes admission formalities.
6. **Billing / Insurance:** Desk handles estimates, deposits, approvals, and final settlement.
7. **Discharge:** Includes prescription, follow-up instructions, reports, and billing clearance.

## 9. Insurance, Billing & Payment Policies

- Cash, credit/debit card, and supported digital payment methods (UPI/net banking) are accepted.
- Insurance/cashless treatment is subject to active insurer/TPA agreements and pre-authorization approval.
- Patients should confirm coverage, exclusions, co-payments, room-rent limits, and pre-authorization requirements before planned admission.
- Initial estimates are indicative; actual charges may vary based on clinical requirements and diagnostics.
- Patients should always request itemized bills and receipts for all payments made.

## 11. Hospital Policies & Code of Conduct

- **Privacy:** Patient privacy and confidentiality are strictly maintained under healthcare standards.
- **Recording:** Photography and video recording inside clinical and patient areas are prohibited.
- **Safety:** Visitors must follow infection-control, hygiene, and security instructions.
- **Prohibited Items:** Smoking, alcohol, and prohibited substances are strictly banned on hospital premises.
- **Conduct:** Aggressive, disruptive, or unsafe behavior may result in visitor restrictions or removal.
- **Medical Authority:** Clinical decisions are made by qualified healthcare professionals based on patient health needs.
- **Charges:** Service availability and prices may change; the billing desk is the authoritative source for final charges.

## 12. Emergency Guidance

For a medical emergency: Immediately contact the hospital emergency desk at **+91-00000-00001** or emergency services (911 / 108) and seek urgent hospital care. Never delay critical medical treatment while waiting for appointment confirmations.
